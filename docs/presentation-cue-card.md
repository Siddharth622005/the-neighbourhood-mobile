# The one-page cue card

Read this once before you walk in. Don't read it on stage — if you need it
on stage, use the thread below, not this whole page.

---

## The thread (say this in your head if you blank)

**Mission. Five, not one. A hundred before the first picture. Only what
applies to you. You's the proud one. We killed Parent Mode. A hundred
forty-two and thirty-plus. The three of them. Mission again.**

Nine beats. Each one below is a full section of the talk.

---

## 1. MISSION (open)

Say the line exact, then pause a beat:

> "The Neighbourhood exists to turn everyday moments into meaningful
> childhood memories, with as little friction as possible."

Then: two months. A website and an app.

---

## 2. FIVE, NOT ONE

Cue: **Home is one of five.**

- Home · Community · Ask · Child · You
- Home is the daily habit. The other four are why it stays open.

---

## 3. THE WEBSITE (100 before the first picture)

Cue: **Most of the work isn't in any of these pictures.**

- Four headlines before this one stuck. Three of them in one sitting, day one.
- **100 commits had already landed before the first screenshot even
  existed.** 142 by the live version.
- At one point three site versions ran side by side. One commit retired
  two of them.

---

## 4. ONBOARDING (only what applies to you)

Cue: **Six questions, but nobody sees all six.**

- Name, role, birthday, birth type, feeding, gender.
- A father skips two. An older child's parent skips one more.
- Birthday still matters most, everything downstream comes from it.

---

## 5. LIVE DEMO (You's the proud one)

Cue: **Walk the phone. Don't talk over a loading screen.**

- Home → Child → You → Ask.
- You gets its own half of the app. Not an afterthought.

---

## 6. SIX DECISIONS (we killed Parent Mode)

Cue: **The fifth one is the whole story. Slow down there.**

- Fewer questions · only what's used · call by name · one hand · **cut
  Parent Mode** · trust shown, not told
- Parent Mode felt like good engineering. Parents never found it. It's a
  tab now.

---

## 7. THE NUMBERS (142 and 30+)

Cue: **Say the headline number, then go straight to what's half done.**

- 1,149 activities · 137 milestones · 54 vaccines · 17 tables
- 142 commits on the website, confirmed. 30+ on the app, a floor not a
  ceiling, some shipped straight through Vercel.
- Working vs half done. Say it straight, don't apologise for it.

---

## 8–10. THE THREE

- **Yati** — aggressive as a compliment, everyone's a little scared of
  her, never needs chasing.
- **Muskan** — brutally honest, sent Rachit her actual medical reports as
  proof nobody asked for.
- **Tejas** — national-level table tennis, the founder's nephew, works
  hard anyway.

---

## 11. CLOSE (mission again)

Same line as the open. Say it, then stop talking:

> "Everyday moments into meaningful childhood memories, with as little
> friction as possible."

It's just a link. Thank you.

---

## If you genuinely blank on stage

Say the thread out loud, slowly, as if it's the whole talk in miniature:

> *"Mission. Five, not one. A hundred before the first picture. Only what
> applies to you. You's the proud one. We killed Parent Mode. A hundred
> forty-two and thirty-plus. The three of them. Mission again."*

Then pick whichever beat you remember most and start talking about that.
The audience doesn't know the order you planned, they only know the order
you deliver.
