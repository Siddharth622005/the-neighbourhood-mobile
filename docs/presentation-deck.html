What we're building

The app tells you four things to do with your child today.

  • One for moving — crawling, climbing, balance.
  • One for talking — words, songs, taking turns.
  • One for thinking — sorting, hiding, figuring out.
  • One for feelings — sharing, comfort, naming moods.

Four cards. A few minutes each. You do them, you tick them off, you're done for the day.

Where it stands
Working

Sign-up, the daily four, discoveries, vaccinations, the parent side, the AI, community. Real accounts and a real database.

Half done

Reports, the kit guide and the product guide are built and clickable, but the content isn't written yet.

Needs a doctor

The vaccination list is copied carefully from the official schedules, but a paediatrician has to check it before anyone relies on it.

Not started

No analytics, so I can't tell you yet whether people come back on day two. No payments. The waitlist has 11 sign-ups.
